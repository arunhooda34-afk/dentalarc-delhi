Common Questions

We've Got Answers

Everything you need to know before your first visit to DentalArc.

Is dental treatment painful at DentalArc?
Absolutely not. We use the latest local anaesthetic techniques combined with a gentle, patient-first approach. Most patients are surprised at how comfortable procedures feel. We also offer sedation options for patients with severe anxiety.
Do you accept health insurance?
Yes! We accept all major health insurance providers including Mediassist, Cigna TTK, Star Health, HDFC ERGO, and more. Our front desk team will help you process cashless claims seamlessly.
How should I prepare for my first visit?
Just arrive 10 minutes early to fill a brief health questionnaire. Bring any previous dental X-rays if you have them, and a list of any medications you're currently taking. No other preparation is needed — we'll take care of the rest.
How long does a teeth whitening session take?
In-clinic professional whitening takes approximately 60–90 minutes for a full session. You'll typically see results 3–4 shades brighter immediately after your first session.
At what age should my child first visit a dentist?
We recommend the first dental visit as soon as your child's first tooth appears, or no later than their first birthday. Early visits help us spot issues before they develop and build positive associations with dental care.
Do you offer payment plans for expensive procedures?
Yes. For procedures like implants and orthodontic treatment, we offer interest-free EMI options through partner banks including HDFC, ICICI, and Bajaj Finserv. Ask our front desk for details.
What should I do in a dental emergency?
Call our emergency line immediately at +91 98101 45678. We provide 24/7 emergency consultation over the phone and can arrange an urgent visit. For knocked-out teeth, keep the tooth moist in milk or saliva and call us right away.
How often should I get a dental check-up?
For most adults, a professional cleaning and check-up every 6 months is ideal. Some patients with gum disease or a history of cavities may benefit from more frequent visits — we'll advise based on your specific situation.
